About

I'm Oscar, the founder and filmmaker behind Neubauer Studios.

I'm based in Nelson, and I started out making brand films for local businesses. The projects I kept coming back to were the ones with real people at the centre, a couple's wedding day, a room full of people who showed up for something that mattered.

So that's what Neubauer Studios is built around. I shoot most projects myself, which means you deal with one person from the first conversation to the final film, and I bring in help when a day needs it. I stay close enough to catch what actually happens, and far enough back that you forget the camera is there.

Whether it's a wedding, an elopement or an event for your business, the aim is the same: a film that still holds up years from now.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you travel outside Nelson?+

Yes, we travel wherever your day takes us. Let us know your venue and we'll confirm availability.

How far ahead should we enquire?+

As early as you can, particularly over summer. We only take a limited number of full wedding days each month.

When do we receive our completed collection?+

Typically within 1-2 weeks, depending on the season. You'll get a private link to review your completed collection of edited films and final assets before anything is final.

Do you work alongside a photographer?+

All the time, we're used to working alongside a photographer so neither of us ends up in the other's shot. If you don't already have a photographer, we can sometimes recommend and pair you with one to create a combined photography and videography package, where available.
